## Unreleased

### 2026-10-12

- Omitted the old checkout's local `.cmd` launch wrappers and screenshot capture workflow from the public source release; the README documents standalone game and server launch commands instead. The generated `build/` runtime remains excluded from Git and source distribution.
- Prepared the standalone `pmflex` source repository with the Promod license, original credits, build, install and local launch instructions, and a clean history containing only the public mod. A fresh build linked without errors or warnings; standard/profile switching, economy, and platform readiness passed on an isolated local runtime. The optional server extension passed its public-stub, S&D, HQ, native-event and contract-mismatch checks from this source build.
- The public match-stat recorder is now a safe no-op interface; its ledger, event encoding and online delivery remain in the private CoD4x server extension. The public copy of the managed `platform.gsx` adapter was removed. Ordinary servers still build and play offline. A separately packaged version function and build sidecar detect an incompatible private override. Stock platform-ready and standard suites and private S&D/HQ/native fixtures passed locally; no hosted server or public client release was changed.
- The S&D, HQ and native statistics test commands now share one private runner; the HQ command forwards the CoD4x executable and the native reducer reads the evidence file returned by that runner. The mod build reuses its `mod.ff` hash in both manifests.

- The economy buy phase length is a server setting, `promod_buy_seconds` (seconds, limited to 10-60; unset keeps 30). `promod/economy.gsc` `configuredBuySeconds()` reads it at the start of every buy phase, so a server config or rcon change applies from the next round without a client update. The buy countdown, the loadout header and the tests' live-round waits all follow `level.economyBuySeconds`. The economy smoke test checks the default 30, the 10 and 60 limits, that a change does not shorten the running buy phase, and that the next buy phase uses 15 seconds; the combined, repeat-buy, platform-ready, S&D/HQ match-stats and flex S&D suites passed.
- The economy sniper armor penetration upgrade costs $150 instead of $100 (`level.economyPrices["sniper_penetration"]` in `promod/economy.gsc`). Armor stays $50 a charge, so armor is common from the first round while the upgrade costs more than one round's $100 income: a sniper saves, gets a kill or takes a teammate's $50 gift first, and cannot pay for it every round on income alone. The buy menu's price chip, purchase and funds messages, refunds and auto-rebuy read the configured price. The penetration fixture now funds the purchase itself (one round's income no longer covers it), checks a funds rejection at $149 and the $150 debit, refund and duplicate; the repeat-buy fixture funds the sniper case from the configured price. The combined and repeat-buy suites pass.
- The economy Frag costs $100 instead of $50 (`level.economyPrices["frag"]` in `promod/economy.gsc`). The buy menu's price chip, purchase and funds messages, refunds and auto-rebuy all read that price, so a Frag plus a red dot or the sniper upgrade now needs $200. The strat-mode preview of the flat menu also charges $100 for a Frag instead of a fixed $50. The economy, combined, repeat-buy and UI-lab fixtures now expect $100 (`frag_price_100`, the $100 purchase, refund and rebuy, and the preview going from $550 to $450) and pass.
- Car explosions are halfway back to original Promod. Every car handled by `maps/mp/_destructible.gsc` deals 225 damage at the centre falling to 10 at the edge (PromodFlex had 150 to 0, Promod 300 to 20). The `vehicle_80s_sedan1_` sedans get their larger blast back at about 306 units (7.8 m), halfway between Promod's 375 and PromodFlex's 6 m (236 units); the other cars use about 243 units (6.2 m), halfway between Promod's 250 and 236. By the linear falloff a sedan is lethal to a player with 100 health and no armor within about 4.5 m (PromodFlex 2 m, Promod 6.8 m) and another car within about 3.6 m (PromodFlex 2 m, Promod 4.5 m). These distances are calculated, not measured in game; the combined suite loads the changed script on Crash without errors.
- Economy matches no longer kick players with "EXE_SERVERCOMMANDOVERFLOW". In a live economy match a remote player was kicked three times while the server ran 130-160 reliable commands ahead of a client limit of 128. Every second during the buy phase, and all round for anyone whose buy menu counted as open, each player's menu refreshed, and every refresh re-sent the class limits to all players, the teammate panel to every teammate and the economy state to every player: about 300 commands per client per second with 10 players. Test clients never close the menu, so with bots it never stopped. Now the once-a-second update only runs while a menu is open and sends the buy countdown, plus the cash and the teammate panel when they changed (at most once a second); a full refresh happens when the menu opens and after the player's own actions, and sends the panel to that player only. The server marks menus closed when the buy phase ends and no longer opens the buy menu for test clients (CoD4x GUID `0`, or an empty GUID with a `bot` name, as stock test clients have; local LAN humans also have an empty GUID). A class change re-sends only the Demolition slot to that team instead of the whole economy state to everyone, class limits are sent only when they change, and a teammate gift updates only the two players. The economy state is now 3-4 batched commands instead of about 13, the teammate panel 6 instead of 19, and the 18 server dvars on connect 2 instead of 18 sent 50 ms apart.
- Verification for the entry above: a scratch build counting every script call that queues a reliable command ran 10 test clients on the stock dedicated server, two of them acting as humans (menu open all buy phase, buying, refunding, gifting and changing class). Per client and second during the buy phase: 304-310 on average before (446 in the round-start second) and 1.6-8.2 after (58 at most, in the round-start second); during the live round 285 before and under 1 after. Server-wide about 3,050 per second before and 30 after. Engine-generated commands (config strings, obituaries, chat) are not counted. The combined (now also checking that a test client's menu is not held open), platform ready-up, S&D and HQ match-stat, flex S&D, repeat-buy, standard and UI-lab suites pass. On a local CoD4x 21.2 server with the platform bot kit (9 clients, economy `flex_snd12`) every client was admitted, both rounds started with all 9 alive, no buy menu was opened for the test clients (an earlier kit run with the home server's pre-change files opened 18) and there were no script errors. Not yet played by a remote human.

### 2026-10-11

- New PromodFlex Search and Destroy ruleset, `promod_mode flex_snd12` (load with `g_gametype sd`), for ranked 5v5. It applies Promod's match-mode rules (ready-up at the start and at halftime, a 6-second strat time before every round, no knife round), then MR12: sides switch after 12 rounds and the first team to 13 wins. At 12-12 the match goes to one MR3 overtime: the teams stay on their sides for 3 rounds, switch, and the first to 4 overtime rounds (16 in total) wins. The overtime side switch does not run a ready-up (only the strat time), so a player who is missing at round 27 does not stop the match; the economy resets at the start of overtime and at its halftime, like a half. A 15-15 after overtime ends the match without a winner, which the match recorder reports as a no-contest (unranked), as it already did for a tied S&D match; there is no second overtime. The HUD reads "PromodFlex MR12", plus "OT" in overtime. The PromodFlex matchmaker switches S&D to this ruleset with a separate migration, after clients and servers have this patch. Match mode also brings Promod's other match rules to S&D: no dead chat during rounds, the post-death delay, and team timeouts.
- Verification for the entry above: a new fixture (`tools/test-flex-snd.ps1`, `promod/flex_snd_smoke.gsc`) drives real round ends on an isolated dedicated server with jumped scores and passed 42 checks: the MR12 settings, strat time on every round it reached, the halftime (with ready-up), overtime at 12-12 with the same sides and no ready-up, the overtime halftime without a ready-up, and match ends at 15-15, 16-13 and 13-8, each followed by a fresh MR12 match. A 10-client run of the platform bot kit on a local CoD4x server with plugin 0.5 loaded `flex_snd12`: all 10 admitted, the held player blocked the start, three live rounds each with strat time and all 10 alive, `match_start` reported `flex_snd12`, no script errors. The combined, platform ready-up and S&D/HQ match-stat suites still pass. Overtime has only been reached by the fixture, not by players.
- Match-mode servers on CoD4x no longer print "Server Violation: Extra IWD Files Detected" every two seconds because of CoD4x's own `xbase_00.iwd` and `jcod4x_00.iwd`, which a CoD4x server lists in `sv_iwdNames` (seen on the local CoD4x 21.2 test server). This affected HQ 2v2 matches and would have affected `flex_snd12`, the first S&D mode in match mode. Source change; the message itself is shown to players and was not observed in a client.
- Match stats now report wallbangs and distance. Every `damage` fact carries `"penetration"` (the bullet went through a surface: `iDFlags & level.iDFLAGS_PENETRATION`, now passed from `Callback_PlayerDamage`) and `"distanceM"` (attacker to victim at the hit, in whole metres: `int(distance * 0.0254 + 0.5)`, capped at 1000). A credited `death` carries the same two fields for the killing hit: the victim remembers its last enemy hit (cleared on spawn), and if the killer is not the one who landed it, the distance is measured at death and penetration is false. World deaths carry neither. The PromodFlex matchmaker rejects unknown fields, so its `stats-wallbang-distance` change (which accepts them and counts wallbang kills, long-range kills and the longest kill) must be deployed before servers run this build; otherwise the plugin drops whole event batches and the match statistics are quarantined. The S&D recorder fixture now checks both fields on a damage fact with and without the penetration flag, that the death fact repeats the killing hit, and that a world death has neither; the S&D and HQ fixtures passed on an isolated dedicated server. Not yet seen in a human match, so no real wallbang has been recorded yet.
- Platform S&D matches let players spawn every round again. Each S&D round is a map restart, and the match-stats round went live in the restart's first frame, before clients came back; the platform spawn guard (no spawns into a live limited-life round) then kept every player dead for the round. The round now goes live once every player admitted earlier in the match has respawned, or when the 4-second grace period ends; players who rejoin later still wait for the next round. Who to wait for is kept in `game["matchStats"]["present"]` (set on join, cleared on disconnect), which survives the restart. HQ is unchanged. Found with a 10-client local S&D run of the platform bot kit: before, both observed rounds started with nobody alive; after, both listed all 10. The S&D and HQ match-stat fixtures and the platform ready-up suite pass. Not yet played by humans.
- Match stats no longer raise a script error at round start when the ready-up or knife-round flags were never set (`level.rdyup`, `game["PROMOD_KNIFEROUND"]`); they count as off.
- Backlot, Crash, Vacant, Citystreets, Strike and Crossfire are easier to read. Their wall, ground and prop textures have half the contrast (less rust, grime and speckle competing with players), grass and foliage are greener so the ground separates from walls, and the containers' grooves are softer. Player models, weapons, HUD art and vehicles are unchanged. Each map also gets a PromodFlex vision (`vision/pmflex_<map>.vision`), applied at game start and every round start: Backlot's is less tan with a little less lift and contrast; Crash, Citystreets and Strike only have a less warm highlight tint; Vacant and Crossfire keep their stock grade. The textures replace stock images through `pmflex.iwd`, which grows from about 0.5 MB to 46 MB. Generated by `tools/map-visuals/` (see its README). Built into an isolated runtime and captured on all six stock maps in the mod with Promod's client settings, before and after at matched views; not yet played in a match.
- The economy buy phase lasts 30 seconds instead of 20. It runs before the strat-time countdown, so each economy round starts 10 seconds later. Standard mode has no buy phase. The tests' waits for the live round now follow the buy length, and the local economy runner allows 60 seconds. The combined suite and the local economy test passed with 30 seconds.
- New match option `promod_reduced_frag 1`: frag grenades (`frag_grenade_mp`, `frag_grenade_short_mp`) deal 65% of their damage (at 60% of the blast radius a frag does about 96 instead of 148, so it no longer kills from full health). It is read once per map, like the red-dot option, and scales the damage before friendly fire, armor, stats and the damage log see it, so all of those report the reduced number; C4 and other explosives are unchanged. The PromodFlex match vote can now choose it for S&D (a FRAGS ballot in the launcher, plugin 0.5 sets the dvar); Headquarters always uses full frag damage. When it is on, the match-stats `match_start` fact carries `"reducedFrag":true`; it is left out otherwise, so standard matches stay valid for a backend that predates the field. The combined economy suite now checks it on an isolated dedicated server: a 40-damage frag hit took 26 health with the option on and 40 with it off, both frag weapons were scaled, and C4 was not; the S&D and HQ match-stat recorder fixtures also passed. Not yet tried in a human match.

### 2026-10-10

- The "Record Demo" popup at ready-up no longer opens on servers that record every player themselves (CoD4x `sv_autodemorecord 1`, as on the home servers). Source change; build and the combined and standard suites pass, but no match-mode ready-up has been run with it.
- Match-mode servers no longer print "Server Violation: Modified Custom IWD File While In Match Mode" and "Extra IWD Files Detected" every two seconds. The server check (`promod/servercheck.gsc`) compared `z_c_r.iwd` against upstream Promod's checksum, but PromodFlex rebuilds that file with the HQ 2v2 ruleset, and it did not recognise PromodFlex's `ui_style.iwd`; both are now accepted without a checksum, like `pmflex.iwd`. Seen in a live HQ match on a home server whose `mod.ff` predates the PunkBuster-check removal, which is why that message also appeared. Source change only; not yet run on a match server.
- The main menu About panel now shows only the title, the codpromod.com link and "PromodFlex v0.1 (based on Promod LIVE V2.20)", centered in a shorter panel. The "Maintained by Ingram" line, which read as if Ingram maintained PromodFlex, the former-developers line and the thank-you line were removed; Promod LIVE credits remain in `README.md` and `LICENSE.md`.
- Players no longer see "Server Violation: Punkbuster Disabled". CoD4x has no PunkBuster, so the Promod server check (`promod/servercheck.gsc`) printed it on every PromodFlex server; the other server checks are unchanged.

- Controls > Multiplayer Controls now has a rebindable "Buy Menu" entry (`openscriptmenu loadout open`), at the top of the Promod group. Players previously had no way to bind it except the console.

- The in-game HUD shows the armor vest icon with the remaining charge count to the left of the grenade icons while the player holds armor charges (`economy_armor_charges`). On connect to a server without the economy, the value is cleared so a previous server's charges are not shown.

Filled class slot squares use the class-name gold instead of white, and the "White slots: taken / Dark: available" legend and the small "BUY CLOSED" label in the Lethal panel are removed. The menu is held to 255 items: an earlier draft with more items failed to link.

- The buy-phase hint now names the key the player has bound to the buy menu (`[{openscriptmenu loadout open}]`, the same key substitution Promod uses for its `[{+activate}]` hints) instead of a fixed "F6". The buy menu footer no longer lists "F6 Buy / Loadout" or "F7 UI Lab", because menu text has no key substitution. The default key itself is set by the PromodFlex launcher (branch `launcher-buy-menu-bind` in `cod4-promod`): F6, or F7 when F6 is taken, only when no key already opens the buy menu. The build linked without errors or warnings and the combined economy checks passed; the rendered hint, including what CoD4 shows for an unbound command, has not been seen in a human client.

- Loadout menu gear row: Frag and the tactical grenade now share one GRENADES box (left half buys Frag, right half swaps flashbang/smoke, each with a hover highlight and a small action line), and armor moved from the strip under the panel into a narrow ARMOR column to its left: one click buys one charge, the two vests fill from the bottom, and the price chip (hidden when buy is closed) and the charge count sit in the column. Without the economy the armor column is hidden and the grenades box spans the full width. Frag and armor clicks while buy is closed or before spawning now reach the server's purchase check, which reports the same messages the separate closed/inactive click areas did.

- Hovering a loadout control now shows a one-line description in the footer bar in place of the key help (attachments, camo, weapon tiles, Frag, tactical, armor, auto-rebuy, teammate cards, Deploy / Save & Close). "REPEAT: ON/OFF" is renamed "AUTO-REBUY: ON/OFF", with matching status messages. The menu is at 245 items. The build linked without errors or warnings, and the combined economy checks and the repeat-buy checks passed on an isolated dedicated server; the layout, hover behavior and the widened standard-mode grenades box have not been seen in a human client yet.

- Loadout purchases now have their own sounds: `ui_purchase` (`sound/promod/ui/purchase.wav`) plays on every successful buy (Frag, armor, attachments including suppressors, sniper upgrade, Demolitions), and `ui_denied` (`denied.wav`) replaces the stock "player left" sound on every refusal (no cash, buy closed, wait for spawn, unavailable class or gift).
